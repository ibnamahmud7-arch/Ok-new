About

Friend Manager v2.0

All friend data stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Why "unfriend" is manual: Facebook does not provide any API to unfriend people. This app opens the profile in Facebook so you can unfriend there, then records it locally after you confirm. It never claims a friend was removed without your confirmation.

We never see, ask for, or store your Facebook password.
